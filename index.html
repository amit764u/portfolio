PROFILE

Science with a purpose.

Connecting veterinary practice, biomedical research, and practical innovation to address real-world health and rural-development challenges.

Dr. Amit Kumar Saxena is a veterinary professional with postgraduate training in Veterinary Science and an MBA in Finance. His professional background includes government veterinary service and research/project roles associated with CBME, IIT Delhi and an IIT Delhi–AIIMS collaboration.

His interests include One Health, animal disease surveillance, functional-food research, AI-enabled monitoring, biotechnology, and technology-supported rural development.
